# UX Validation

UX validation examines how well a person can accomplish a specific task in a product. It documents the experience and explains which improvements would benefit the user.

## Language

**UX validation**

A scoped review that attempts a task in the running product from the intended user's perspective and examines both the outcome and any friction along the way.

**Validation run**

One attempt to validate the UX of a scoped task, with its recorded outcome, walkthrough, and findings.

**Figma audit report**

An editable presentation of a validation run that includes the task outcome, findings grouped by category, supporting evidence, and an Action Plan.

**Action Plan**

The section of a Figma audit report that organizes proposed next steps from a validation run into phases.

**Walkthrough**

An ordered record of the actions taken to attempt a task and the product states observed along the way.

**Finding**

A specific strength, problem, or idea grounded in walkthrough evidence that explains why it matters to the intended user.

**Finding category**

The label for the kind of finding and the response it calls for. The categories are Good!, Needs Improvement, Idea, and Needs to be Fixed.

**Needs Improvement**

A problem that adds avoidable friction while the task remains reliably achievable.

**Needs to be Fixed**

A problem that prevents reliable task completion or creates a consequential error.

**Priority**

A ranking of issues by their impact on the task under review. The four levels are P0 (Stops the task), P1 (Serious obstacle), P2 (Avoidable effort), and P3 (Minor friction).

**Evidence**

An observation from the walkthrough that supports a finding, such as a captured screen or a recorded interaction.

**Evidence highlight**

A mark on a screenshot that identifies the part of the interface supporting a finding.

**Evidence card**

A numbered note beside a screenshot that identifies the finding and shares its number and category with the corresponding evidence highlight.

**Hypothesis**

A proposed explanation or user impact that goes beyond what the walkthrough directly established.
