# The owner always selects the design

The factory also produces designs: editable frames in the project's Figma file. Unlike planning, where the owner is involved only by exception, every piece of design work stops for **Selection**. Exploration produces several Variations, the Council reviews and ranks them, and the owner keeps one, combines parts of several, asks for more Exploration, or rejects them all. Refinement starts only after that decision. The Council may recommend a Variation but never selects one, however well it scores, because a design score measures craft against a rubric, while choosing a direction is a matter of taste and product judgment that belongs to the owner. As in planning, no run waits for the owner. The Exploration run ends, and the owner's change on the Factory ticket starts Refinement.

## Considered Options

- Let a high Council score select automatically, the way a Gate decision lets a Plan be implemented: rejected because the owner wants creative control over direction, and an unchecked rubric score would only make the factory confident about the wrong design.
- One Variation, with the owner reviewing only the finished design: rejected because the owner decides by comparing alternatives, and refining one direction before anyone has chosen it wastes rounds on a design that may be thrown away.
