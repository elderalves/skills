# UX Validation

UX validation examines how well a person can accomplish a specific task in a product. It documents the experience and explains which improvements would benefit the user.

## Language

**UX validation**

A scoped review that attempts a task in the running product from the intended user's perspective and examines both the outcome and any friction along the way.

**Walkthrough**

An ordered record of the actions taken to attempt a task and the product states observed along the way.

**Finding**

A specific issue in the reviewed experience that identifies what should improve and explains why it matters to the user.

**Evidence**

An observation from the walkthrough that supports a finding, such as a captured screen or a recorded interaction.

**Hypothesis**

A proposed explanation or user impact that goes beyond what the walkthrough directly established.
