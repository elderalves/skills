# Glossary Map

## Contexts

- [UX Validation](./skills/ux-validation/GLOSSARY.md): scoped usability reviews of one task in a running product, and their reports
- [Software Factory](./docs/factory/GLOSSARY.md): agents that turn Upstream tickets into plans, reviewed decisions and draft PRs, with a human involved only by exception, and into Figma designs whose direction the owner always selects

## Relationships

- **UX Validation ↔ Software Factory**: independent; no shared terms
- **Software Factory → `software-factory` skill**: the factory reuses that skill's Task, Trigger, Run, Runner and Orchestrator vocabulary
